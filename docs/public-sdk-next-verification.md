## Remaining identifier conversion contracts

The six pure conversion declarations are at pinned `plugins/function_declarations.h:299–304` and aliases at `src/asmsource/pluginexports.asm:61–65`. Current `native_stats_probe.inc::identity_probe` calls them through a loaded rebuilt C DLL; the native identity suite supplies valid/invalid syntax, private-universe classification and an independently derived GUID golden value. Pure identifier conversion does not authenticate an official Steam or CoD4x account.

Do not promote the whole conversion group from the aggregate `conversions=1` marker. [Build 73's actual result](../results/plugin-identity-contract-native-20261003-074025-734500/result.json) passed in 12.4725 seconds. PID 2016 on port 28990 unloaded the module and exited normally after its own `quit`; no forced termination was needed. The archive, server, rebuilt DLL, package, source, independent oracle, and native logs are hash-bound. The harness suite has ten passes and one host-skipped actual symlink-creation case; mocked reparse and other confinement/refusal cases passed. Those supplementary harness tests are not export semantic assertions.

Each classifier actually passed **32,838** independent calls: 32,768 assembled universe/type/instance/account cases, 64 independent bit flips, and six literal goldens. `Plugin_SteamIDIsIndividual` permits universe1 or32–39; `Plugin_SteamIDIsIndividualAndSteamAccount` permits only universe1. Both require type1 and instance1 and ignore low account bits. These are pure numeric classifications; they establish no official identity or authentication.

The same actual run passed 99 selected-format, 96 explicit-decimal, and 128 Steam3 checks. The loaded DLL switched both synchronized runtime dvar modes, checked guard bytes, minimum/overflow capacities and type/universe spellings, then restored its original default-one setting. The decimal formatter preserves pinned empty-buffer behavior below twenty bytes; twenty-digit values need twenty-one bytes to avoid the pinned overflow. The per-export candidates bind `native/steam_identity_format.hpp`, actual helper-suite results, wrappers/control scope, compiled C fixture and current DLL. A separate startup explicitly configured zero was **not** rerun on Build 73, and archived save/restart persistence remains untested. The historical Build 71 configured-zero phase cannot substitute for those missing current-binary dvar cases.

`Plugin_GUID2PlayerID` passed **247** calls: seven independently derived Python/.NET goldens in three case variants, five invalid-hex classes at every position, all wrong lengths0–64, null input, and a valid terminated prefix. The compiled fixture also checks caller-buffer preservation. Valid32-hex input retains the pinned256-byte salt, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256/100, little-endian account and private-universe layout. Its current evidence remains partial: local cryptographic-provider failure was not injected. Strict invalid-hex/null rejection and zero on provider failure are documented safety adaptations to pinned partial `strtol` parsing, null dereference and private-ID/account-zero fallback, not a claim that all error paths were executed.

`Plugin_StringToSteamID` is still pending and its production semantics were not changed in this batch. Its next complete compatibility fixture must cover account-type aliases (`T`, `c`, `L`), allowed universes, default/explicit instances, field-leading ASCII whitespace and optional plus signs in pinned positive forms, and negative/overflow/junk refusals. Preserve those valid pinned forms, remove the undocumented bracketed-decimal extension, and handle documented four-field Steam3 conversion-count correction only after source/fixture review. The parser's safety corrections must be recorded individually rather than inferred from the classifiers or formatter results.

Remaining identity work is current-binary configured-zero/persistence coverage, actual provider-failure injection, and the parser compatibility matrix.
